## Diagnosis by layer
- Wrong server, nothing listening: the query times out (no DNS reply at all). No IP is obtained,
  so no TCP/TLS/HTTP traffic happens. Fix: point the client at 10.7.9.180.
- Wrong server, public resolver: a reply arrives but NXDOMAIN, because only Mac 1 knows team1.test.
- Missing record: Mac 1 answers NXDOMAIN (local=/team1.test/ means it never forwards that zone).
  curl fails at name resolution (exit 6) before any connection attempt.
